Denver, Colorado - September 7th, 2023 - AWOL Vision, the industry leader in home entertainment innovation, is set to unveil its latest creations at this year's CEDIA Expo, taking place from September 7th to 9th in Denver, Colorado. The company will debut the LTV-3000 Pro and LTV-3500 Pro laser projectors, both boasting Dolby Vision and Control4 integration. Additionally, the spotlight will shine on their award-winning Vanish TV, recently honored with the "Best of IFA 2023" award by Screen Rant and MUO.
